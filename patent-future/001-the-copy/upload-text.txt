Dad died three years ago. Every Sunday, he still comes to dinner. He just drinks motor oil now.

In File 001 we look at the Copy: a machine that could keep a person alive as a digital copy. What do today's grief bots already do? How far is science from copying a whole brain? And if your copy wakes up while you are still alive, which one is really you?
